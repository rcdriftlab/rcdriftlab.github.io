RC Drift Lab – Privacy Policy

RC Drift Lab by Stranger Kreations · Last updated November 22, 2026

RC Drift Lab is a free browser game. You can play without an account. This page explains what is stored if you choose to sign in or play online.

Playing without signing in

Your settings, garage setups and personal best laps are saved in your own browser (local storage). They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

If you sign in with Google

Sign-in is handled by Google and our backend provider, Supabase. We receive your Google account ID and email address so we can create your game account. We do not get your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

With an account we store:

  • The driver name you choose (shown publicly on leaderboards and in rooms)
  • Your saved car setups and looks
  • Your lap times and scores per track (best laps are shown publicly on leaderboards)

Online rooms

When you join an online room, your driver name and your car's position are shared with the other players in that room so they can see you on track. Car data travels peer-to-peer between players' browsers (WebRTC), which means other players' browsers can see your IP address, as with most online games. Rooms are not recorded.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no ad tracking
  • We don't sell or rent your data
  • We don't share your email with other players

Donations

Donations go through Venmo. We don't see or store your payment details; Venmo's own privacy policy applies.

Deleting your data

Email unfathomabledesigns@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete your account, driver name and lap history. To clear local data, clear this site's storage in your browser.

Kids

RC Drift Lab is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
